## Provenance and caveats

- **The two PDK PDFs come from `libs.doc/doc/` of the PDK**, renamed to lower case to match the folder convention. Both are byte-identical to the copies the PDK ships as of 2026-08-25 (`IHP-Open-PDK @ bf898e9`, the PDK in the IIC-OSIC-TOOLS 2026.08 image): layout rules **Rev. 0.4 (2024-12-19)**, process specification **Rev. 1.2 (2023-12-20)**. The revision is on the title page of each document. A repo copy of a PDK document ages silently, so check that stamp against `$PDK_ROOT/$PDK/libs.doc/doc/` before letting one of these decide a rule.
- **The layout cheatsheet is a convenience, not a rule source.** Its resistance columns assume bulk-aluminium resistivity and come out roughly a factor of two optimistic against the `RESISTANCE RPERSQ` values in `sg13g2_tech.lef`, and its "min. Width" column lists 0.42 µm for TopMetal1 and 0.2 µm for TopMetal2 where the tech LEF says 1.64 µm and 2.0 µm (0.42 is the TopVia1 cut size). Take widths from the DRC deck, resistance from the tech LEF or from PEX, and the DC current limits from the tables in the [LibreLane cheatsheet](librelane/librelane_cheatsheet.md), which are read out of the tech LEF. The sheet's `Contact` current limit of 0.3 mA per cut exists only there, the tech LEF has no `DCCURRENTDENSITY` entry for `Cont`.
- **Nothing in `doc/` is generated by a make target.** It is all hand-maintained.
